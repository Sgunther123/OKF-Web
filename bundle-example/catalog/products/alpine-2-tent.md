# Overview

The Alpine 2 is a freestanding, double-wall, two-person tent for
three-season backpacking.[^pdp] It has two doors and two vestibules,
so each occupant gets their own entrance and gear storage.[^pdp]

# Specifications

* Packed weight: 1.9 kg (4 lb 3 oz); trail weight without stakes and
  stuff sacks: 1.7 kg.[^spec-sheet]
* Floor area: 130 × 220 cm (2.86 m²); peak height: 105 cm.[^spec-sheet]
* Fly and floor: 20-denier ripstop nylon with 1,500 mm polyurethane
  coating; inner: no-see-um mesh.[^spec-sheet]
* Poles: DAC Featherlite NSL aluminum, single hubbed unit.[^spec-sheet]
* Packed size: 46 × 15 cm.[^spec-sheet]
* Includes 8 aluminum stakes, 4 guylines, and repair sleeve; the
  matching footprint is sold separately.[^pdp]
* Country of manufacture: Vietnam.[^spec-sheet]

# Fit and use notes

The store recommends this tent for pairs prioritizing low weight over
interior space, and notes it is not intended for winter or exposed
alpine conditions.[^pdp]
