# Washing

Machine-wash in a front-loading washer only, cold, on a gentle
cycle, using a down-specific soap — never household detergent, which
strips the down's natural oils.[^care-page] Tumble-dry low with two
or three clean tennis balls until every clump of down has broken up;
this can take multiple cycles.[^care-page] Never dry-clean the
bag.[^care-page]

# Storage between trips

Store uncompressed, in the large cotton storage sack the bag ships
with — long-term compression permanently reduces loft and
warmth.[^care-page] The compression sack is for the trail, not the
closet.[^care-page]

# Small repairs

Patch small shell tears with a nylon repair patch over the cleaned
area; no sewing needed, and it keeps down from escaping.[^care-page]
The store's repair service handles zipper and baffle work beyond
that.[^care-page]

# Warranty help

The Ridgeline 20 is covered by the store's limited warranty against
defects in materials and workmanship; wear, misuse, and moisture
damage from storage are not defects.[^care-page] To start a claim,
use the warranty page on the store site; you will receive a claim id
and status token by email.[^care-page] Agents can then check
progress with the
[warranty claim status lookup](/capabilities/warranty-claim-status.md)
— the claim itself is filed by the owner on the store's own pages,
never by an agent.[^care-page] A wrong-size exchange is a
[returns](/policies/returns.md) matter, not a warranty
claim.[^care-page]
