# Season rating

All tents currently sold are three-season models, built for spring
through fall conditions; the guide advises against using them in
sustained snow load or high alpine winter conditions.[^guide]

# Choosing capacity

The guide recommends sizing up when campers are taller than 183 cm,
camp with a dog, or keep packs inside the inner tent.[^guide] The
[Alpine 2 Tent](/catalog/products/alpine-2-tent.md) fits two standard
sleeping pads (each 51 cm wide) side by side with no spare
width.[^guide]

# Weight vs. livability

Freestanding tents pitch faster and hold shape on rock or platforms,
at a small weight cost compared to trekking-pole shelters.[^guide] The
guide positions the Alpine 2 as the store's all-round pick for pairs
counting grams.[^guide]
