# Overview

The Ridgeline 20 is a mummy-style down sleeping bag for three-season
backpacking.[^pdp]

# Specifications

* Temperature ratings (EN 13537): comfort -1 °C (30 °F), limit -7 °C
  (20 °F).[^pdp]
* Fill: 650-fill-power duck down, 540 g of fill in the regular
  length.[^pdp]
* Total weight: 1.1 kg (regular), 1.2 kg (long).[^pdp]
* Sizes: regular (fits to 183 cm) and long (fits to 198 cm).[^pdp]
* Shell: 20-denier ripstop nylon with DWR finish; full-length snag-free
  zipper.[^pdp]
* Packed size: 20 × 33 cm in the included compression sack.[^pdp]

# Care

The store advises storing the bag uncompressed in the included storage
mesh sack and machine washing only with down-specific soap.[^pdp]
