# Payments

The store accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and
Apple Pay.[^faq] Payment is captured when the order ships.[^faq]

# Support

Support is email-only at support@alpinepeakoutfitters.example, with
replies within one business day on weekdays.[^faq]

# Sizing help

Sleeping bag length recommendations appear on each product page; when
between sizes, the store recommends the longer size.[^faq]

# Gift cards

Digital gift cards are available in 25–500 USD amounts, are delivered
by email, and do not expire.[^faq] Gift cards are not refundable (see
the [Return & Exchange Policy](/policies/returns.md)).[^faq]
