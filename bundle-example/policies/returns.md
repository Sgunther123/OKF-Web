# Window and condition

Returns are accepted within 60 days of the delivery date.[^policy]
Items must be unused, in original packaging, and with tags
attached.[^policy] Tents and sleeping bags may be set up or unpacked
indoors to inspect them, but items showing outdoor use, dirt, or odors
are not returnable.[^policy]

# Exclusions

Gift cards and items marked "final sale" cannot be returned.[^policy]
Opened fuel canisters cannot be returned or shipped back.[^policy]

# Refunds and exchanges

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business
days of the warehouse receiving the return.[^policy] Exchanges are
processed as a return plus a new order.[^policy] Return shipping within
the United States uses a prepaid label with a flat 7 USD fee deducted
from the refund; the fee is waived when the return is due to a store
error or a defect.[^policy]

# How to start a return

Customers start a return from the order page linked in their
confirmation email or by contacting
support@alpinepeakoutfitters.example.[^policy]
