# Destinations

Orders ship to all 50 US states and to Canada.[^policy] The store does
not currently ship to other countries.[^policy]

# Costs

Standard US shipping is 6.95 USD and is free on orders over 75
USD.[^policy] Expedited US shipping (2 business days) is 19.95
USD.[^policy] Shipping to Canada is a flat 24.95 USD; Canadian orders
may owe duties and taxes on delivery, which are the customer's
responsibility.[^policy]

# Timelines

Orders placed before 12:00 US Mountain Time on a business day dispatch
the same day; later orders dispatch the next business day.[^policy]
Standard US delivery takes 3–6 business days from dispatch, and Canada
delivery takes 6–12 business days.[^policy] Fuel canisters ship only by
ground within the contiguous United States.[^policy]

# Carriers and tracking

Shipments use UPS or USPS, chosen at dispatch, and every order receives
a tracking link by email.[^policy]
