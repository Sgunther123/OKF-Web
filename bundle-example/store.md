# Overview

Alpine Peak Outfitters is an independent online retailer of
backpacking and camping equipment, founded in 2014 and operated from
Boulder, Colorado, USA.[^about] The catalog focuses on tents, sleeping
bags, and camp accessories selected for three-season backcountry
use.[^about]

# Reaching a human

Customer support is available by email at
support@alpinepeakoutfitters.example and answers within one business
day on weekdays.[^contact] There is no phone support.[^contact]

# What this store is not

Alpine Peak Outfitters does not operate physical retail stores and
does not sell through third-party marketplaces.[^about]
