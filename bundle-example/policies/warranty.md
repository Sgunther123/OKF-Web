# Coverage

Alpine Peak–branded products are warranted against defects in
materials and workmanship for two years from the delivery
date.[^policy] The warranty covers manufacturing defects and does not
cover normal wear, UV degradation, or damage from misuse.[^policy]

# Remedy

The store's stated remedy is repair first; items that cannot be
repaired are replaced with the same or an equivalent product.[^policy]
Warranty claims start by emailing
support@alpinepeakoutfitters.example with photos and the order
number.[^policy]

# Third-party brands

Products from other brands are covered by the manufacturer's own
warranty, and the store will facilitate claims during the first 60
days.[^policy]
