# Sleeping bag length — Ridgeline 20

| Your height | Size to order |
| --- | --- |
| up to 183 cm (6'0") | Regular |
| 183–198 cm (6'0"–6'6") | Long |

Regular fits sleepers to 183 cm; long fits to 198 cm.[^bag-pdp]
Exactly between sizes, or you sleep in extra layers? Order the
long — the store's standing recommendation.[^size-guide] Above
198 cm the Ridgeline 20 is not offered in a fitting length; the
store states this plainly rather than recommending a workaround.[^size-guide]

# What fits inside the Alpine 2

The Alpine 2's floor is 130 cm wide by 220 cm long.[^tent-spec]

* Two standard sleeping pads (51 cm / 20") fit side by side with
  room to spare.[^size-guide]
* Two wide pads (64 cm / 25") fit side by side exactly — 128 cm of
  the 130 cm floor — with no gap at the walls.[^size-guide]
* Two Ridgeline 20 bags (regular or long) fit side by side; the
  long's 198 cm fit-height leaves clearance within the 220 cm floor
  length.[^size-guide]
